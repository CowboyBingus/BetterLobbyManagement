Better Lobby Management v1.0
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.
Tested with a host and one friend: DISBAND SQUAD and PROMOTE work. Squads of
three or four have not been tested.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer and import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2. Keep the shared
loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement. Mod Options Menu is optional.
The mod is also an option of Vanilla Plus Megapack; enable only one copy.

Only the host needs Better Lobby Management. The other players need no mod.

When you host a squad on your ship, the escape menu's GAME tab (the one
listing the squad) gets two buttons under the game's own. Each opens the
game's confirm dialog (hold to confirm):

- DISBAND SQUAD: posts "The host disbanded the squad." in the chat, then
  kicks every other player with the game's own KICK; they return to their
  own ships.
- PROMOTE <name>: every player sees the game's own line "<name> is the new
  squad leader", then <name> is kicked to their own ship and hosts it, and
  you and the rest of the squad join them there. You stay in the squad. Keep
  the game running until the squad has moved; the log says when it is done
  or why it stopped, and 15 seconds later how many of the squad arrived.

Keep the escape menu open until the kicked players are gone: the mod kicks
with the game's own player-menu KICK, so a player's menu may open for a
moment. After PROMOTE the escape menu closes by itself once the new host has
left.

<name> is the player whose card you selected last; before that, a friend of
yours if there is one, otherwise the player with the lowest id. The kicked
player sees the game's "kicked" message. A successor whose lobby is Friends
Only or Invite Only refuses a squad that is not on their friends list; the
squad then stays with you.

Only planned promotions are covered: if the host crashes or quits the game,
the squad disbands as it does today.

The Galactic Map's lobby scanner recharges in 5 seconds instead of the
game's 20, so the squads on the globe refresh sooner. Every scan is a lobby
search; 5 seconds is the shortest setting.

Options (Mod Options Menu):
- Squad Messages: On (default) or Off.
- Scanner Recharge (Seconds): 5 (default) to 20, never longer than the
  game's own.
- Lobby Region: My Continent Only limits the Galactic Map scanner and
  quickplay to lobbies hosted on your continent. It makes no extra
  searches; fewer lobbies may be listed.
